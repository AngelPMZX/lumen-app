## Full description (4000 chars max)

```
Lumen is your emotional gym: a few minutes a day to understand yourself better, calm your mind and build habits that take care of you. No rush, no guilt, and with Lumi, a little drop of light, by your side.

🌱 A GARDEN THAT GROWS WITH YOU
Every lesson, check-in and challenge earns you seeds. Plant clover, bamboo, a cherry tree or a lotus, care for them with boosters and harvest rewards. Your garden reflects how much you have looked after yourself.

📚 9 WELLBEING PATHS, 90 LESSONS
Emotions, self-knowledge, mindfulness, resilience, self-esteem, relationships, self-love, anxiety and stress, and sleep. Short lessons of 5 to 7 steps with quizzes, scenarios, myths and facts, guided practices and micro-challenges for your day. Content grounded in well-known practices such as cognitive behavioural therapy, written carefully and without magical promises.

📝 A PRIVATE EMOTIONAL JOURNAL
Write like in a real notebook: warm paper, ruled lines and a different prompt each day when you do not know where to start. Log your mood with 12 emotions and see your week at a glance. Only you can read it.

🫁 GUIDED BREATHING
Three techniques (box 4-4-4-4, calm 4-7-8 and gentle flow), from 1 to 10 minutes, with an orb that guides you, sound cues for each phase and ambiences to keep you company: rain, forest, slow waves, a lullaby and more. Lumi breathes with you.

🔥 STREAKS, MEDALS AND MISSIONS
A daily streak that never punishes you, weekly missions with their chest, a daily review of what you learned and 22 bronze, silver and gold medals to see how far you have come.

📊 YOUR WEEKLY SUMMARY
Every Sunday, a summary with your dominant mood, your personal patterns (for example, how you feel on days you write) and a recommended lesson. Everything is calculated on your phone.

🤝 HELP WHEN YOU NEED IT
Lumen includes free helplines by country (Mexico, Colombia, Argentina, Chile, Spain and the United States), always one tap away, always free.

✨ MADE WITH CARE
· Light and dark mode.
· Reduce motion option.
· Built with screen readers in mind.
· Our own gentle sounds, nothing harsh.
· Spanish and English.
· No ads.

🔒 YOUR PRIVACY, SERIOUSLY
Your journal and moods are private: nobody else sees them and they are never sold or shared. Usage statistics are anonymous and never include what you write or how you feel. You can delete your account and all your data from the app, anytime.

IMPORTANT: Lumen is a wellbeing and emotional education app. It is not medical or psychological care and does not replace a professional. If you are going through a crisis, please reach out: the free helplines for your country are inside the app.

Made with care by TheDarking Studios.
```
