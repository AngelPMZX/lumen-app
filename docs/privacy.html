Last updated: September 17, 2026 · Applies to: the Lumen app (com.thedarkingstudios.lumen) on Android, iOS and web.

Lumen is an emotional wellbeing app: lessons, a journal, habits, guided breathing and a garden that grows with you. What you write here is yours. This policy explains, in plain words, what is stored, why, who it is shared with and how to delete it.

Important: Lumen is not medical or psychological care and does not replace a professional. If you are in crisis, please reach out: inside the app, Profile → “Do you need help now?” lists free helplines by country.

1. Who is responsible

TheDarking Studios (individual developer, Mexico), responsible for the Lumen app. Contact: lumen.app.soporte@gmail.com.

2. What data is stored

DataWhyBasis
Account: name, email, username and, if you provide them at sign-up, age, gender, interests, music genres and the archetype they produce. To create your account, let you sign in from any device and personalise the app. Performance of the service you asked for.
Your wellbeing: mood check-ins, journal entries (text and tags), habits and their check-ins, reminders, lesson and review progress, challenges, breathing sessions, garden and missions. To make the app work: your progress, streak, weekly summary and recommendations. Performance of the service. This is sensitive health data and is treated as such.
App usage (anonymous): which screens are opened and which actions happen (for example “lesson completed”), with content ids. To know which parts are used and improve the app. Legitimate interest in improving the product.
Crashes: crash reports with device model, OS version and the technical detail of the error. To fix bugs. Legitimate interest in a stable service.
On your phone: preferences (theme, sound, reduce motion) and the journal draft if you leave without saving. So the app behaves as you left it and you do not lose what you were writing. Performance of the service. It never leaves your device.

3. What is not collected or done

  • Your data is never sold or rented.
  • There are no ads or ad networks inside Lumen.
  • Your location, contacts, photos and microphone are not collected.
  • Usage statistics never include which mood you logged, what you write in the journal, your exercises or your visits to crisis support.
  • Nobody on the team reads your journal; database rules only allow access to your own account.

4. Who it is shared with

Only the services that make the app work, as processors:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics): account and app data. Firebase privacy.
  • Google Sign-In, only if you choose to sign in with Google: it shares your name and email to create the account.
  • Google Play, for app distribution and, in the future, in-app purchases.

Data may also be shared if legally required by an authority.

5. Where and for how long

Data is stored in Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure, encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. It is kept while your account exists. If you delete your account, your app data is removed; crash reports and anonymous statistics may be kept for up to 14 months in aggregate form, without identifying you.

6. Your rights

  • See and correct: your information lives in the app; you can edit your name and username in Profile → Edit profile.
  • Delete everything: Profile → Edit profile → Delete account. Your progress, moods, journal, habits, reminders and garden are deleted and your account is closed. It is immediate and cannot be undone. You can also request it by email: how to delete your account.
  • A copy of your data or objection: write to lumen.app.soporte@gmail.com and we reply within 30 days.

7. Minors

Lumen is intended for people aged 13 or older (16 in European Economic Area countries where that age applies). If you are younger, please do not create an account. If we find an account belonging to someone younger, we will delete it.

8. Security

Your account is protected with a password (at least 8 characters, with letters and numbers, and no very common passwords) or with Google. Each person can only read and write their own data; the database is configured that way. Still, no system is perfect: if we detect a breach that affects you, we will notify you by email.

9. Changes to this policy

If something changes, this page and the date above will be updated. If the change is significant, we will tell you inside the app.

10. Contact

Questions, requests or reports: lumen.app.soporte@gmail.com.
